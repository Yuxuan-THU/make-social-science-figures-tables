# Evidence base and scope

## Official sources

- AJPS, *Guidelines for Figures and Tables in Final Drafts*, updated April 9, 2026: https://ajps.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Guidelines-for-Figures-and-Tables_4_9_2026.pdf
- APSA Style Manual, *Tables & Figures*: https://connect.apsanet.org/stylemanual/sample-page/tables-and-figures/
- APSR, *Research Transparency*: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/information/journal-policies/research-transparency
- AJPS, *Guidelines for Accepted Articles*: https://ajps.org/guidelines-for-accepted-articles/

## Local corpus used to infer production defaults

- 100 formally published main-text excerpts: 25 APSR figures, 25 APSR tables, 25 AJPS figures, 25 AJPS tables.
- 29 papers represented: 15 APSR and 14 AJPS, primarily 2021–2026.
- Figure coverage: coefficient/forest, marginal/predicted, event study, time series, distribution, scatter/binned scatter, bar/composition, map, network/flow, and design/theory.
- Table coverage: regression, descriptive/sample, balance, treatment effect, model comparison, measurement/coding, crosstab/classification, case/qualitative evidence, and formal theory/simulation.
- No family exceeds 25% of its artifact corpus; DOI+number and perceptual-hash checks are required.

The corpus is a private local scholarly-review library and is not part of this portable skill. Official rules are distinguished from corpus-derived implementation defaults in each journal profile.
